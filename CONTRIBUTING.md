# Contributing to Joe

Bug reports, research notes, reproducible test cases, and design discussions are
welcome through the official repository.

Joe is currently preparing its long-term contribution framework. To preserve a
clear chain of title and the option of future commercial licensing, acquisition,
or dual licensing, external code contributions are not merged until the
contributor has accepted a contributor agreement approved for the project.
Opening a pull request by itself does not transfer copyright and does not imply
that the contribution will be merged.

Until that agreement is published, please open an issue before investing in a
code contribution. The project owner may implement the idea independently or
invite a narrowly scoped contribution. Do not submit code, data, model weights,
or other material that you do not have the right to contribute.

The contributor agreement should be reviewed by qualified counsel before Joe
begins routinely accepting external code. Its purpose will be to keep
contributors as authors of their work while granting the project owner the
rights needed to distribute, sublicense, relicense, enforce, and transfer the
combined project.
